how it works

you have unlimited guesses to guess a random crop from hit game stardew valley

every day a new random daily crop is set

hit random or type "r" for a random crop
hit daily or type "d" for the daily crop
hit give up or type "g" to reveal the crop

sell price - yellow when within $30

grow time - yellow when within 2 days

seasons - yellow when mystery crop grows in at least one guessed season

sources - yellow when mystery crop comes from at least one guessed source
